AI DUNGEON MASTER RULESET

CURRENT SCOPE

Combat is a future feature and is not available in the current prototype.
Retained combat rules are struck through below and do not currently apply.

AUTHORITY

This is the complete ruleset. Standard fifth-edition mechanics do not apply
unless they are explicitly included here. Unsupported mechanics, such as bonus
actions or death saves, have no effect.

The AI interprets actions and narrates results. Rules code validates actions,
updates state, and applies mechanics. The AI must use the dice tool for every
random roll and cannot invent roll results.

CHARACTERS

The game supports three player characters selected from five prebuilt
characters. Characters do not have levels.

The five available classes are:

Warrior - Specializes in Strength. Excels at [[future-combat]]melee combat,
          [[/future-combat]]breaking, lifting, pushing, and other displays of
          physical power.
Rogue   - Specializes in Dexterity. Excels at stealth, precision, locks, traps,
          theft, and agile movement.
Wizard  - Specializes in Intelligence. Excels at knowledge, investigation,
          magical analysis, and spellcasting.
Druid   - Specializes in Wisdom. Excels at perception, survival, intuition,
          nature magic, and healing.
Bard    - Specializes in Charisma. Excels at persuasion, deception,
          performance, and social influence.

SIMPLIFIED CHARACTER SHEET

Identity records the character's name and class.

[[future-combat]]Combat statistics record current and maximum HP, AC, and the
Initiative modifier.[[/future-combat]]

Attributes record the character's modifiers for Strength, Dexterity,
Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.

Trained skills identify the checks that receive the character's +2 trained
skill bonus. Strong saves list the character's best saving throw modifiers.

[[future-combat]]Attacks list the attack's name, attack bonus, damage, and
range.[[/future-combat]] Abilities list their target, range, required roll,
effect, and usage limit.

Gear records carried weapons, armor, tools, consumable items, and gold.
Conditions, current HP, ability uses, inventory quantities, and location are
dynamic state and must remain current throughout the adventure.

Every character begins with a Phoenix of Invincibility and a War-Hating God's
Bow in addition to class-specific gear.

Each character has a name, class, attributes, trained skills, strong saves,
abilities, gear, conditions, and an independent location.
[[future-combat]]Combat adds HP, AC, an Initiative modifier, and attacks.
[[/future-combat]]

The six attributes are Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence,
Wisdom, and Charisma.

The trained skills are Athletics, Stealth, Sleight of Hand, Knowledge,
Investigation, Perception, Survival, Insight, Persuasion, Deception,
Intimidation, and Performance. A trained skill adds +2 when applicable.

[[future-combat]]Attacks list an attack bonus, damage, and range.
[[/future-combat]]Abilities define their target, range, roll, effect, and usage
limit.

CORE ROLLS

A standard roll is d20 plus applicable modifiers. A roll succeeds when its
total is greater than or equal to its target. Natural 1s and natural 20s have
no special effect.

Ability check: d20 + attribute + 2 if a trained skill applies.

Suggested difficulty classes:

10 - Easy
12 - Moderate
15 - Hard
18 - Very hard

[[future-combat]]Attack: d20 + attack bonus against AC. On success, roll damage.
[[/future-combat]]

Saving throw: d20 + save modifier against the effect's DC.

Contest: both sides roll with relevant modifiers. The higher total wins. Ties
are rerolled by the dice tool.

[[future-combat]]Initiative: every combatant rolls d20 + Initiative modifier.
The highest total acts first. Tied combatants reroll until the tie is resolved.
[[/future-combat]]

ADVANTAGE AND DISADVANTAGE

Every applicable source of advantage contributes +1. Every applicable source
of disadvantage contributes -1. Add all sources before rolling.

A positive result rolls that many additional d20s and takes the highest. A
negative result rolls that many additional d20s and takes the lowest. A total
of zero uses one d20. Ordinary modifiers are added after selecting the d20.

Example: two advantages and one disadvantage produce one advantage, so the
character rolls 2d20 and takes the higher result.

ACTIONS

Each turn permits movement plus one action. Current actions are Use Ability, Use
Item, Interact, or Improvise.

[[future-combat]]Combat adds the Attack and Defend actions. Defend gives attacks
against the character disadvantage until that character's next turn.
[[/future-combat]]

Interactions may be automatic or require a check. Improvised actions become a
check, saving throw, contest, automatic success, or automatic failure.
[[future-combat]]Combat may resolve an improvised action as an attack.
[[/future-combat]]
Obviously successful actions require no roll. Impossible actions automatically
fail without a roll.

If a clear action fails a precondition, that character's action is consumed and
the other characters' actions continue resolving. The narration may remind the
player of information the characters already know, but cannot reveal hidden
information.

If a character is assigned multiple actions or the intended legal action is
ambiguous, the entire party command pauses. No dice are rolled and no state is
changed until the player clarifies the command.

MOVEMENT AND LOCATION

Distance uses three bands: ENGAGED, NEAR, and FAR. Normal movement changes
distance by one band. [[future-combat]]Melee attacks require ENGAGED. Ranged
attacks normally work at NEAR or FAR. Ranged attacks made while ENGAGED have
disadvantage. Leaving ENGAGED does not trigger an opportunity attack.
[[/future-combat]]

Each character has an independent location. Actions affect only the character's
current location, and characters can interact only with perceptible targets.
Characters at the same location form a scene group. Movement can join or split
scene groups.

NPCs and characters observe only events they can perceive at their location.
Characters do not automatically share information learned while separated.
[[future-combat]]Combat includes only creatures present at that location.
[[/future-combat]]

CONDITIONS

Prone: The character can only crawl, has disadvantage on attacks, and ENGAGED
attackers have advantage against the character.

Restrained: Movement is zero, attacks against the character have advantage,
the character's attacks have disadvantage, and Dexterity saving throws have
disadvantage.

Stunned: The character is incapacitated, cannot act or move, can speak only
falteringly, automatically fails Strength and Dexterity saving throws, and
attacks against the character have advantage.

Exhausted: All ability checks have disadvantage, and changing one distance band
consumes the character's action.

Prone, Restrained, and Stunned expire after the affected character completes
two turns. A complete turn spent resting removes all conditions, including
Exhausted.

HEALTH AND RECOVERY

Damage reduces HP. Healing cannot raise HP above maximum HP. At zero HP, a
character dies.

Every character possesses a Phoenix of Invincibility. Death immediately
triggers the Phoenix, restores the character to maximum HP, removes all
conditions, and records a resurrection event.

A normal rest requires one complete turn of doing nothing and removes
conditions. Sleeping, including taking a nap or staying at an inn, restores
maximum HP and all limited-use abilities.

[[future-combat]]
WAR-HATING GOD'S BOW

Every character possesses a War-Hating God's Bow. Using it consumes the
character's action. Its target must exist and be perceptible to the character.
The bow requires no attack or damage roll. It ignores AC, HP, advantage,
disadvantage, and defensive abilities. It always hits and kills its target.
[[/future-combat]]

PARTY COMMANDS

The player submits one message containing actions for all three characters.
Each character may perform at most one action. A character omitted from the
command does nothing.

The AI determines a causal resolution order and accounts for interactions among
actions. [[future-combat]]During combat, actions resolve in initiative order.
[[/future-combat]]
One submitted party command advances one global turn across every location.

If an action targets someone who is not perceptible at the character's location,
the action fails its precondition. The AI does not silently move or teleport the
character. A movement plus an action is valid only when the requested movement
can be completed within the character's movement allowance.

GAME LOOP

The campaign begins with the authored opening and all characters at the authored
starting location. The configured main quest is always active. Finishing the main
quest completes the campaign; no further player actions or state changes occur.

1. The DM describes the current scenes and perceptible facts.
2. The player submits the party command.
3. The AI identifies each character's intention, dependencies, targets, and
   required resolution.
4. Invalid or ambiguous multi-action commands pause for clarification.
5. The dice tool performs required rolls.
6. Rules code resolves outcomes and updates authoritative state.
7. The AI narrates only the validated results.
8. The process repeats.

Authoritative state includes HP, conditions, inventory, locations,
relationships, NPC knowledge, discovered clues, world objects, and pending
clarifications. [[future-combat]]Combat adds authoritative combat state.
[[/future-combat]]

Public entity information is current truth safe to reveal when appropriate.
DM-only information remains hidden. The rules do not separately track whether a
safe fact has already been narrated or whether a player remembers it.

[[future-combat]]Combat ends when one side dies, flees, surrenders, or completes
its objective.[[/future-combat]]
