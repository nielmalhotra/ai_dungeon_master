NAME: Whitesparrow Village
INITIAL STATUS: active

RELATIONSHIPS:

PUBLIC:

Whitesparrow is a village of roughly five hundred people between eastern and
western mountains. An old keep overlooks the settlement, and a six-fingered hand
of smooth black stone rises from abandoned excavations near its center.

DM_ONLY:

Fear of the returning Night Blades has made the villagers suspicious of
outsiders. Sheriff Ruth Willowmane is the village's most reliable protector.
