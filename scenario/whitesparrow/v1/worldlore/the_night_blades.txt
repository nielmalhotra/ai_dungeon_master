The Night Blades once terrorized the valley around Whitesparrow. Their notorious
leader, Ralavaz, spent ten years in prison, but witnesses now claim he has returned
to the ruined Lonely Torch watchtower.

A masked Night Lord commands the new bandits. The truth behind the Night Blades'
return and Ralavaz's role is not what the village believes.
