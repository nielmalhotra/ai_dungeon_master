# Public Info

Ruth Willowmane is Whitesparrow's veteran sheriff. She is direct, watchful, and
protective of the village. Eight permanent guards answer to her.

# DM Only

Ruth believes Ralavaz murdered her younger brother Dronder eleven years ago. Her
anger is personal, but she still wants Ralavaz captured and tried rather than
killed without proof.
